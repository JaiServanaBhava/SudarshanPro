## 🤔 Why Sudarshan Pro?

You shouldn't need to install a mobile app, create an account, or forward ports just to control your own PC from your phone. **Sudarshan Pro** is a single Windows executable that turns any smartphone browser into a full remote — no phone app, no cloud, no sign-up. Scan one QR code and you're in.

> 🎯 **Made for real life:** classrooms with smart boards, home media PCs, office kiosks — anywhere you want the PC to obey your phone instead of the other way around.

## 🚀 Installation & Setup — 20 Seconds

> ✅ **No code. No Python. No dependencies.** You only ever touch one file: `SudarshanPro.exe`.

1. **⬇️ Download** `SudarshanPro.exe` from the badge at the top (or the [Direct Link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PtmbLT0mRky7qzX1UMUDUjovTk7V5yRV/view?usp=sharing)).
2. **▶️ Double-click** it on your Windows PC.
3. **🔳 Allow Firewall** — when Windows asks, tick **both** *Private* and *Public* networks so your phone can reach the PC.
4. **📷 Scan the QR code** shown on the PC screen with your phone's camera.
5. **🔑 Enter the PIN** — the default is `1234`. *(Change it immediately via **🔑 Change PIN** in the phone UI for full security.)*

**That's it.** Your phone is now the remote. 📱🖥️

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I need to install anything on my phone?**
No. The whole remote runs in the phone's browser.

**Q: Does it need the internet?**
No — phone and PC talk directly over your local Wi-Fi.

**Q: Where do transferred files go?**
Into the **DropZone** folder on the PC's Desktop. List & download everything from the phone UI.

**Q: How do I stop it?**
Tap **🛑 Close Sudarshan Pro on PC** at the bottom of the phone UI — or just close the window on the PC.

**Q: Is it safe to use on a shared network?**
Use it on a network you trust, change the default PIN before first real use, and close the app when the PC is unattended.
